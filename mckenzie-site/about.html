Meet our leadership team.

Matt McKenzie
PhD, LPC-S, NBC-HWC
Owner & Clinical Supervisor

Matt McKenzie is the Owner, Clinical Supervisor, and Continuing Education Training Consultant at McKenzie Strategies. He also serves as a professor at The University of Alabama, where he teaches in the graduate counseling program. Matt holds a doctoral degree in Counselor Education & Supervision and is an active Licensed Professional Counselor (LPC) in Alabama and Georgia. He is a Member and Certified Trainer with the Motivational Interviewing Network of Trainers (MINT), an AL First Responder Clinician/Trainer, and a Board Certified Health & Wellness Coach. Matt brings over a decade of counseling experience and has spent many years presenting workshops on group counseling, motivational interviewing, and compassion fatigue. Outside of work, he enjoys spending time with his family and friends and competing in Brazilian jiu-jitsu.

Meghan McKenzie
MA, LPC, NBC-HWC
Owner & Therapist

As we move through life, we collect seeds: joy, pain, change, transition, grief, and trauma. Carried around, they take up space. Planted and cared for, they can grow into something new. Through telling your story — the good and the not-so-great, my hope is to help you find the tools to grow your own garden.

I work with individuals and couples navigating grief, trauma, life transitions, relationship challenges, and anxiety. As an integrative therapist I take a person-centered approach, which means you're the expert on your own life and we move at your pace. In sessions I draw on Motivational Interviewing to help you find your own reasons for change, Family Systems theory to understand how the people around you shape your story, and Narrative therapy to help you separate who you are from what you've been through. I have completed Level 1 Training in Gottman Method Couples Therapy, which guides my work with couples, and I hold a Trauma-Informed Yoga certification, so I can offer grounding, body-based tools to clients who want them.

Page Rubin
MSW, LICSW-S, PIP
Therapist

Page Rubin has close to two decades of experience working with adults and adolescents. She specializes in substance use disorders, anxiety, depression, and personality disorders. Whether a client is questioning their relationship with alcohol or other substances, struggling with persistent worry or low mood, or working to change long-standing patterns that strain their relationships and emotions, Page meets them where they are.

Page uses a person-centered, strength-based approach. Rather than focusing only on what's wrong, she helps clients recognize the strengths, values, and resources they already have, and builds on them to create lasting change. She believes a trusting relationship is the foundation of effective therapy, so she works to create a space where clients feel heard, respected, and free from judgment. That matters most for people who have felt judged or misunderstood in the past.

Lindsey Smith
MA, ALC
Therapist

Lindsey has a master's degree in Counseling with a concentration in Marriage, Couples and Family Counseling from UAB. She has been clinically working at Children's of Alabama as a Certified Child Life Specialist for over 11 years and did her counseling internship at UAB hospital, broadening her knowledge through counseling geriatric and palliative care clients. Lindsey has taken great value in working with patients and families undergoing childhood cancer and has expertise in emotionally caring for and serving parents, staff members, and families who have experienced trauma, grief, and loss in a vulnerable setting. She specializes in helping children, adolescents, teenagers, adults, geriatrics, parents, couples and families with a vast range of presenting concerns such as relationship issues, crisis intervention, grief and loss, depression and anxiety. With an integrative approach using Family Systems theory, Person Centered and Gestalt therapy, Lindsey provides a warm, empathetic, and strong focus on the therapeutic relationship built with her clients.

Supervised by Dr. Matthew McKenzie, PhD, LPC-S, MINT

Shannon McCarthy
PhD, ALC, NCC
Therapist

Shannon has a master's degree in clinical Mental Health Counseling and a PhD in Counseling and Counselor Education with a concentration in Marriage, Couples, and Family Counseling from the University of Florida. She has been in the counseling field for over 17 years, and is currently an Associate Professor in the counseling program at UAB, where she has been a full-time faculty member for 11 years. Kimberly Kirklin

Therapist
