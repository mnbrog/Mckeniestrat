Meet our leadership team.

Matt McKenzie
PhD, LPC-S, NBC-HWC
Owner & Clinical Supervisor

Matt McKenzie is the Owner, Clinical Supervisor, and Continuing Education Training Consultant at McKenzie Strategies. He possesses a Doctoral degree in Counseling Education & Supervision and is an active Licensed Professional Counselor (LPC) in AL and GA. Additionally, Matt is a Member of the Motivational Interviewing Network of Trainers, Certified MINT Trainer, AL First Responder Clinician/Trainer, and a Board Certified Health & Wellness Coach. Matt has 7 years of counseling experience and 5 years of presenting workshops on group counseling, motivational interviewing, and compassion fatigue. He also has experience providing health and wellness coaching/training. He enjoys spending time with his family, friends, and competing in Brazilian jiu jitsu.

Meghan McKenzie
MA, LPC, NBC-HWC
Owner & Therapist

I see life experiences as seeds — some big, some small, some uncomfortable, oddly shaped, smooth; the names of these seeds could be joy, pain, flux, transition, grief, or trauma. As we begin to collect seeds they take up space. Learning how to plant those seeds, foster and care for them, can possibly allow them to bloom. Through telling your story, the good and the not so great, it is my hope as a therapist to help provide the tools you need to sow your own garden. As an integrative therapist I rely on a person-centered approach and utilize methods like Motivational Interviewing, Family Systems theory, and Narrative therapy in my sessions. I also pull from my Gottman Level 1 Couples Certification and my Trauma Informed Yoga Certification when working with my clients.

Lindsey Smith
MA, ALC
Therapist

Lindsey has a master's degree in Counseling with a concentration in Marriage, Couples and Family Counseling from UAB. She has been clinically working at Children's of Alabama as a Certified Child Life Specialist for over 11 years and did her counseling internship at UAB hospital, broadening her knowledge through counseling geriatric and palliative care clients. Lindsey has taken great value in working with patients and families undergoing childhood cancer and has expertise in emotionally caring for and serving parents, staff members, and families who have experienced trauma, grief, and loss in a vulnerable setting. She specializes in helping children, adolescents, teenagers, adults, geriatrics, parents, couples and families with a vast range of presenting concerns such as relationship issues, crisis intervention, grief and loss, depression and anxiety. With an integrative approach using Family Systems theory, Person Centered and Gestalt therapy, Lindsey provides a warm, empathetic, and strong focus on the therapeutic relationship built with her clients.

Supervised by Dr. Matthew McKenzie, PhD, LPC-S, MINT

Shannon McCarthy
PhD, ALC, NCC
Therapist

Shannon has a master's degree in clinical Mental Health Counseling and a PhD in Counseling and Counselor Education with a concentration in Marriage, Couples, and Family Counseling from the University of Florida. She has been in the counseling field for over 17 years, and is currently an Associate Professor in the counseling program at UAB, where she has been a full-time faculty member for 11 years. Alongside her academic role, Shannon is excited to return to clinical work, specializing in helping adolescents, teenagers, adults, parents, couples, and families with a wide range of presenting concerns including relationship issues, grief and loss, depression, and anxiety. She prioritizes creating a supportive, nonjudgmental environment and uses an integrative approach that includes Family Systems theory, Cognitive Behavioral Therapy, and Narrative Therapy, and also pulls from her Level 1 training in Gottman Method Couples Therapy.

Kimberly Kirklin
