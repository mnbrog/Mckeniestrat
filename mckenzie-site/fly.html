The Heart Behind Our FLY Program

FLY: Freedom Lies in You.

FLY was created in memory of Matt McKenzie's father, Johnny McKenzie Jr., who was a United States Marine Corps veteran and Hoover Firefighter.

The Freedom Lies in You (FLY) program provides peer-led support groups, psychoeducation, and individual counseling to veterans, police officers, and firefighters.

Why FLY Exists

The people we serve, by the numbers.

More Police Officers

Police officers die by suicide at a rate of 18 per 100,000 officers — more than twice the rate among the general population, and higher than the rate of officers killed in the line of duty (11 per 100,000).

1 in 5

According to a 2016 study by the US Department of Veterans Affairs, about 20% of veterans experience mental health issues, including depression, PTSD, and anxiety.

Roughly 15%

It is estimated that approximately 15–20% of firefighters experience suicidal thoughts at some point in their career.
