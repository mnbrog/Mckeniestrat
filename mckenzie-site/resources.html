Published Work

Experiential Motivational Interviewing: An Action-Oriented Way of Being

Marc Pimsler and Matt McKenzie marry the concept of experiential counseling and motivational interviewing. Experiential counseling is an approach to counseling that focuses on helping individuals gain insight through mindful exploration of their thoughts, feelings and behaviors. Motivational interviewing is a counseling method that focuses on helping clients explore and resolve ambivalence regarding their behavior goals. By combining these two approaches, practitioners can create true engagement.
