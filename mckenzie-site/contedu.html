What is Motivational Interviewing?

Motivational Interviewing (MI) is an evidence-based approach to counseling that helps individuals explore and overcome ambivalence about change. It is a person-centered, nonjudgmental, and directive style of conversation that helps clients identify their goals, values, and needs.
