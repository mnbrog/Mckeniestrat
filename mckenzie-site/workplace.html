Workplace Mental Health & Wellness Services

Forging Forward, Together

Contact Us

Healthy organizations begin with healthy people.

At McKenzie Strategies, we partner with businesses, schools, healthcare organizations, and community agencies to promote psychological well-being, strengthen resilient teams, and equip leaders with practical tools to support today's workforce.

Our customized workshops and trainings are designed to improve employee well-being, reduce burnout, strengthen communication, and foster healthier workplace cultures. Whether you're looking to support your staff through everyday challenges or respond to a critical incident, our experienced clinicians provide evidence-based education tailored to your organization's unique needs.

Our Services

Where we can help.

Stress Management & Resilience

Help employees recognize the effects of chronic stress while developing practical strategies to improve emotional well-being, productivity, and work-life balance.

Burnout Prevention

Learn to identify the early warning signs of burnout, establish healthy boundaries, and create sustainable habits that support long-term engagement and performance.

Leadership Resilience

Equip supervisors and managers with skills to lead with empathy, navigate difficult conversations, support employee mental health, and build psychologically safe teams.

Employee Mental Health Education

Reduce stigma by increasing awareness of common mental health concerns, recognizing when someone may be struggling, and understanding how to access appropriate support.

Critical Incident Response & Debriefing

Following traumatic workplace events, our clinicians provide compassionate, structured support to help teams process experiences, promote recovery, and encourage healthy coping.

Flexible Training Options

Formats that fit your team.

  • Lunch & Learn presentations
  • One-hour educational workshops
  • Half-day and full-day trainings
  • Leadership retreats
  • Multi-session wellness series
  • Virtual or in-person presentations
  • Customized programs designed specifically for your organization

Whether you're looking to proactively invest in your employees or respond to a unique organizational need, we'll work with you to create a program that aligns with your goals.

Interested in bringing mental health education to your organization? Contact us to discuss a customized training or request a consultation.
